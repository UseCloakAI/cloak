examples, no two the same

The smallest model that does it well.

A hello does not need a 550-billion-parameter model. Each message is routed to the lightest open model that will answer it properly. Try a few.

Not negotiable

One rule. Never harm a human life.

What that rules out

Help with suicide or self-harm, violence against people, weapons that kill at scale, anything sexual involving a minor, and hunting down a private person.

What it does not

Hard subjects, blunt opinions, dark fiction, uncomfortable facts. No lectures and no stacked disclaimers. You are an adult and the rest is your call.

When someone is in danger

The task waits. The Blink answers the person, asks directly, and shows real helplines for their country, pinned above the reply.

Built in twice

The rule lives in every Blink and in the platform around them, so safety never depends on a model behaving.
