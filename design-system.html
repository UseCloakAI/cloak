04 — States

Lift. Sink.Invert.

The shadow is the floor. Hover lifts a control off it by exactly the shadow it gains, press slams it flat onto it — the shadow's far edge never moves. Selected inverts to ink.

Rest → hover
Rest→Hover

translate(-2px,-2px), shadow 2→4 (4→6 on cards) on a spring. Ink buttons turn rust.

Press
Pressed

translate by the resting shadow (2px, 4px on primary) and the shadow drops to none in 90ms. Springs back on release.

Selected
Active

Ink fill, paper text. Settings nav adds a 3px rust bar on the right.

07 — Motion + backgrounds

Quick.Then still.

Physical, not floaty. Controls lift off their shadow on a real spring and slam flat when pressed. Display type is uncloaked, cards rise out of the page, exits sink back in. Then everything stops. All of it respects reduced motion.
