Plain Sight.

The Cloak v3 design language. Cloak is honest AI, so the interface hides nothing: calm surfaces, one accent, real hierarchy and motion that only moves when something changed.

Calm, then clear.

Nothing moves on its own. Things come into focus when they arrive, the orb breathes only while Cloak is working, and every animation answers a change you made.

One accent

Slate neutrals and a single accent per theme. Models differ by shape, not by color.

Press round, hold square

Things you press are pills and circles. Things that hold content are soft squares. Nested corners shrink by the padding.

Real contrast

Muted text is a real ink step, AA on every surface, in both modes. No opacity tricks.

Same parts, everywhere

The landing page uses the app’s own components. If it is on the marketing site, it exists in the product.

Motion

Calm, then clear. Every animation answers a change: something arrived, something was pressed, a state moved. Under reduce-motion everything is instant.

Clear.
Focus

Arrivals come into focus: fade, 14px rise, 8px blur to sharp. --ease-out, 420 to 760ms.

Breath

The orb breathes only while Cloak works. It stops the moment the reply is done.

Grow

Theme and mode changes grow out of the control as a circle, the orb’s shape. 600ms --ease-io.

Brand and voice

The orb

cloak

A flat accent disc, no outline. The wordmark is lowercase cloak in Bricolage 700. The orb is also the page’s big shape: the hero sun, the closing sunrise, the auth panel.

Voice

Do

  • Blunt, short, principled. “Instant. Sharp. No filler.”
  • “Cloak” in third person for behavior, “we” for commitments, “you” for the user.
  • Keep the disclaimers visible. They are part of the brand.
  • Specific, modest environmental claims.

Don’t

  • Em dashes. Use a period, comma or colon.
  • All-caps headings or labels.
  • Emoji, AI-generated imagery, purple gradients.
  • More than one label above headings per three sections.

What changed from v2

Same name, orb, accent, values, IA and copy. New visual language.
