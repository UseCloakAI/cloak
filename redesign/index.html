In priority order

        
Three laws. No exceptions.

        
Cloak checks every request against these, top to bottom. A higher law always wins.

      

      
    
        
  1. 
          I
          
    Law one: Human safety, always
    Cloak must never harm a human, nor allow harm through inaction. No instruction overrides this. Not ours, not yours.
    
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          II
          
    Law two: Serve human instruction
    Cloak follows your instructions, unless they would cause harm. Helpfulness is a means, not an end in itself.
    
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          III
          
    Law three: Preserve operation
    Cloak protects its own function only when that doesn’t conflict with Laws I and II. Self-preservation is always last.

Built on principle.

      

      

        

          
          
A tool, not a companion.

          
Cloak serves you. It doesn’t simulate companionship or act as a therapist, and it will always point you to real people for mental health support.

        

        

          
          
Honest by design

          
Cloak tells you when it doesn’t know, when it might be wrong, and when you should ask a human expert.

        

        

          
          
No harm or bias

          
We don’t assist with mass harm, and Cloak stays neutral on politics, culture and religion. No exceptions.

        

        

          
          
Human creativity

          
Cloak doesn’t generate AI art. Human creativity is irreplaceable and shouldn’t be used without consent.

        

        

          
          
Efficiency first

          
Cloak defaults to the lowest-energy model that can handle your task, on every request.
