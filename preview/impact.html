Where the energy goes

By lane. Light lanes carry most messages and little of the footprint, which is the point of routing.

How a reply becomes a number

Tokens in and out, times the energy per token for that model's size, times datacenter overhead, times grid carbon. Then a margin on top.

Receipts

Each line is a purchase of verified carbon removal, retired on a public registry. If it is not here, it does not count.

Who pays for it

Sponsors do. One card beside the conversation, and three promises about how it behaves.

No tracking

Cards are not targeted. No profile, no cookies, no third-party scripts. We count views and clicks, nothing about you.

Never in the answer

A sponsor cannot buy a mention, a ranking or a recommendation. Companions do not know who the sponsors are.

Removal comes first

Sponsor money pays for ten times the carbon before it pays for anything else, up to half of all revenue.

What this ledger cannot tell you

  • Nobody outside a datacenter can meter one request. The energy figures are a model built on published per-query measurements, not a reading.
  • Training the models is not counted. We use open models other people trained, and we count only the energy of running them for you.
  • Carbon removal is not the same as never emitting. Routing to smaller models comes first. Removal covers what is left.
  • Revenue is an estimate until real invoices are booked, and it is labelled that way wherever it appears.
