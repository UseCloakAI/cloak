The mark

Same name, same ink, same orange. One change: the O woke up. It is a hand-drawn orb with two dot eyes that watches your pointer and blinks. Cloak used to hide things. Now it looks back.

Cloakit looks back
  • The O is the only round thing in a square system. That is the point.
  • It is the sixth shape: the five companions are its family, drawn with the same pen.
  • It never spins, glows or pulses. It looks, blinks and hops when you touch it.
  • At favicon size the O stands alone as the app icon.

Shapes with eyes

Five shapes, two dots and a smile each. Drawn by hand as vector paths and rigged in code. No image files and no generated art. Try the moods.

Why they read as drawn

  • Line boil: the ink shifts about seven times a second, the way hand-inked frames never quite line up.
  • The shadow is hatched with a pen, not filled. Cloak's hard shadow, drawn.
  • The eyes are two ink dots. They lead, the face follows, the body leans last, and the arms and toppers arrive late and overshoot.
  • Talking is driven by real energy: stream chunks, or the audio level when a voice is playing.
  • A hop crouches first, stretches in the air and squashes on landing.
  • Reduced motion gives a still drawing, with nothing lost.

Motion

The interface is blunt so the shapes can be lively. One easing for entrances, a spring for anything you lift or press, and a fast exit. Tap to play.

Voice

Plain words, the answer first, warmth without gush. The companions talk like this, and so does every label in the product.
