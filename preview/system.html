Creatures

Drawn by hand as vector paths and rigged in code. No image files and no generated art. Try the moods.

Why they read as drawn

  • Line boil: the ink shifts about seven times a second, the way hand-inked frames never quite line up.
  • Gouache grain held inside each shape, so fills are never flat.
  • Eyes lead and the head follows. Ears, tails and the yuzu arrive late and overshoot.
  • A hop crouches first, stretches in the air and squashes on landing.
  • Reduced motion gives a still drawing, with nothing lost.

Motion

The interface is calm so the creatures can be lively. One easing for almost everything, three durations, and a bounce reserved for things that are glad to see you. Tap to play.

Voice

Plain words, the answer first, warmth without gush. The companions talk like this, and so does every label in the product.
